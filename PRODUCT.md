# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (user's choice), App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4. Statically generated marketing site in English and Arabic (right-to-left).

## Users

Four confirmed audiences, all weighted equally by the owner:

- UAE residents and expats sorting visas for themselves or family, renewals, typing, and tickets home.
- Visitors coming to the UAE (or their UAE relatives) who need a visit or tourist visa and flights in.
- UAE residents travelling abroad who need Schengen, UK, US and other visas, plus tickets, holidays or Umrah.
- Companies and HR teams that need staff visas, bulk ticketing and document work handled.

They usually arrive with one concrete errand and a deadline, often on a phone, and want to know three things fast: do you do my thing, what do I need to bring, and how do I reach a person.

## Product Purpose

KKN Travels is a visa and travel desk in Sharjah, UAE (Block B, B22-100, SRTI Park), serving customers across the UAE including Dubai. The site exists to turn a search ("UAE visit visa", "flight ticket booking Dubai", "typing services Dubai") into an enquiry by WhatsApp, phone, form or walk-in. Success is a qualified enquiry that already names the service the person needs.

## Positioning

One walk-in desk in Dubai that handles the whole errand: visa processing and airline ticketing first, with typing and travel arrangements alongside. The owner calls the offer "PRO services".

## Operating Context

Visa applications, passports, Emirates ID, airline tickets and boarding passes, attested certificates, typing-centre forms, government portals. Customers send document photos over WhatsApp or bring originals to the office.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Main work, and the site's lead message (owner, 2026-10-02): visa services for all top countries (Schengen, UK, USA, Canada, Australia and more), with early appointments, quick processing, and hotel, flight and insurance bookings; plus UAE-specific visas (Dubai visit visa, Dubai family visa "now available", visit visa for Pakistani nationals). Airline ticketing follows.
- Also offered: typing, holiday packages, Umrah, hotels, travel insurance.
- Paused by the owner (2026-10-02): document attestation and translation. The copy is kept but hidden from the site.
- Not offered (not selected by the owner): company formation and trade licensing.
- Enquiries go to WhatsApp, phone and email. No online payment or booking engine.
- Confirmed contact (2026-10-21): phone and WhatsApp +971 50 642 2967, email business@kkn-travels.com, office Block B, B22-100, SRTI Park, Sharjah. Domain kkn-travels.com.
- Undecided: opening hours; map coordinates; prices; whether IATA accreditation applies.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: KKN Travels.
- No existing logo, colours or type; the identity is designed with the site and a real logo may replace the wordmark later.
- Quality bar set by the owner: the site must read as a premium, expensively made website.
- Standing preference from the owner (2026-10-02): luxury and minimal, corporate and trustworthy, in the familiar register of premium travel companies rather than a themed concept. Imagery is photography of famous travel destinations. No pictures of aeroplanes or aircraft.
- Chosen direction: "Panorama" (a strip of destination photographs, then headline and service index on oyster grey with burgundy accents).

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed: a walk-in office in Dubai, UAE (address not yet supplied).
- Confirmed: the business is brand new.
- Absent, and not to be fabricated: years of experience, customer counts, testimonials, reviews, licence or IATA numbers, prices, processing-time guarantees, team photos.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the errand. A visitor should find their exact service and what to bring before reading anything about the company.
- Say only what is true. A new business sells on clarity and process, never on borrowed track record.
- One step to a person. WhatsApp, call and walk-in are always one tap away.
- Arabic is a first-class edition, not a translation bolted on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic right-to-left parity with English. Readable on low-end phones and slow connections. WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard access.
