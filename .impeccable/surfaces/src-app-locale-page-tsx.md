# Surface brief: home page

Scope: the KKN Travels home page (`src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`), English and Arabic. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: UAE residents, visitors, residents travelling abroad and HR teams, each arriving with one errand. The page must show within seconds that the desk does their errand and put WhatsApp one tap away. Proof on hand is thin (new business, walk-in office in Dubai), so the page proves through clear service detail and document checklists, never through invented track record.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/panorama.png` (chosen by the owner from nine rendered compositions on 2026-10-02). The comp's photographs are regenerated as plates in `public/images/destinations/`. Not to be literalized: the comp's slight paper grain on the ground, which is flat colour in the build.

Owner change after approval (2026-10-02): content, including the photo strip, sits in a centred column at most 1200px wide instead of running edge to edge (reference given: alnaboodahtravel.com). The comp's full-bleed strip no longer governs. Document attestation is hidden, so the index has five entries.

Unresolved: real contact details, opening hours and address; native review of the Arabic copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is a panorama of where you can go, then one plain sentence about what the desk does, with every service one click away. It refuses the single skyline photo with a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Oyster-grey ground, a white navigation band, charcoal grotesque type (Wix Madefor), and deep burgundy for the wordmark, pill buttons and chevrons. Photographs are square-cornered with thin white gaps. Hairline rules separate rows. No icons except chevrons, no cards, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees destinations, reads that visas and flights are sorted at one desk in Dubai, finds their service in the index, and starts on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 69px white navigation band. A full-width strip of five destination photographs (Dubai, Istanbul, a wider Paris at centre, Maldives, Makkah), each with a white place name at bottom left. Below on oyster grey: at left the three-line headline, the lede, a burgundy "Start on WhatsApp" pill and an underlined call link; at right a two-column index of six services with hairlines and burgundy chevrons.

FORM: Panorama, a safer-register grounded candidate picked by the owner; seed key a0571821. Signature interaction: the destination strip, where the photograph under the pointer or keyboard focus widens while the others give way; on phones it becomes a snap-scrolling row.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
